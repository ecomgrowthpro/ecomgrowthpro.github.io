Sound familiar?

These are the problems store owners bring to me most often. Every one of them can be fixed.

Shopify

"I get visitors, but hardly anyone buys."Product pages, trust signals and checkout need work. This is conversion optimization.
"My store looks like every other template."A custom theme and brand styling make your store look credible and memorable.
"My store is slow and full of apps."An app clean-up and speed fixes make pages load fast on mobile.
"I'm spending on ads with nothing to show for it."Better targeting, creative and landing pages, with tracking set up properly.

Etsy

"My listings don't show up in Etsy search."Keyword research, titles, tags and attributes help Etsy match you to buyers.
"I get views and favourites, but few sales."Photos, descriptions, pricing and reviews need to close the deal.
"I just opened my shop and have no sales yet."A launch plan: set up the shop well, optimize the first listings, then drive traffic.
"Etsy Ads are eating my profit."Focus ad spend on the listings and keywords that actually convert.
