Every platform sells differently. I know how each one works.

What works on Etsy can fail on Shopify, and the reverse. Here's what I help businesses achieve on each platform I work with.

Shopify

Best for brands that want full ownership, flexibility and room to scale.

Shopify gives you control over your brand, your customers and your data. I help you set it up properly, customize it to stand out, and keep optimizing it as you grow.

View Shopify services

What I help you achieve

  • A professional store that builds trust
  • Faster pages and better search rankings
  • Higher conversion and order values
  • Marketing that brings repeat customers

Main services

  • Store setup and configuration
  • Theme customization and redesign
  • App setup and integrations
  • SEO, speed and CRO

Etsy

Best for handmade, vintage, craft and digital product sellers.

Etsy brings buyers, but competition is intense. I help your listings rank higher in Etsy search and look good enough to win the click and the sale.

View Etsy services

What I help you achieve

  • More views from Etsy search
  • Listings that turn views into sales
  • A memorable, trustworthy shop brand
  • Smarter Etsy Ads spend

Main services

  • Etsy SEO: titles, tags and attributes
  • Listing copy and photo guidance
  • Shop branding and policies
  • Etsy Ads and promotions

Wix

Best for small businesses that want a flexible site and shop in one.

Wix makes it easy to start, but easy to get wrong. I build Wix sites that look polished, work well on mobile and are set up for search from day one.

View Wix services

What I help you achieve

  • A site that looks custom-made
  • Clean mobile layouts
  • Better visibility on Google
  • Simple ways to take orders and bookings

Main services

  • Wix website and store setup
  • Page design and mobile layouts
  • Bookings, forms and payments
  • Wix SEO configuration

Squarespace

Best for creatives and boutique brands where design leads.

Squarespace is beautiful out of the box. I make it yours, with commerce set up correctly, and marketing tools working from the start.

View Squarespace services

  • A refined, on-brand storefront
  • Correct product, shipping and tax setup
  • Better search visibility
  • Launch campaigns that build momentum

Main services

  • Template customization
  • Squarespace Commerce setup
  • SEO and site structure
  • Email campaigns
