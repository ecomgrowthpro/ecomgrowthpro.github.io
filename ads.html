Ads & email questions

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your products, margins and goals. We'll agree a starting budget you're comfortable with and only increase it once the numbers show it's working.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Your ad spend is paid directly to the platform (Meta, TikTok, Google, Pinterest or Etsy) from your own account. My fee covers the strategy, setup and management.

Do I need a Klaviyo account?

Yes, but Klaviyo has a free plan for smaller lists. I'll set it up, connect it to your Shopify store and build your flows.

Which platform should I start with?

It depends on what you sell and who buys it. Book a call or send me your store, and I'll recommend the best place to start.

Spending on ads but not seeing sales?

Send me your store and ad accounts, and I'll show you where your budget is leaking and how to fix it.
