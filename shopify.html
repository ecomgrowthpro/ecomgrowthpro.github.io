Shopify questions

Can you build my Shopify store from scratch?

Yes. I handle everything: account setup, theme, products, payments, shipping, legal pages and connecting your domain, then show you how to manage it.

I already have a store. Can you just improve it?

Yes. Many clients start with an audit of their existing store, then we work through the fixes that will make the biggest difference to sales first.

Do you work with dropshipping and print-on-demand stores?

Yes. I set up supplier apps like DSers, Printful and Printify, and make sure the store still looks like a real brand customers can trust.

Will you need access to my store?

I'll ask for collaborator access, which Shopify provides for this purpose. You never need to share your password, and you can remove access at any time.

How long does a new Shopify store take?

It depends on the number of products and how custom the design is. You'll get a clear timeline in your proposal before any work starts.
