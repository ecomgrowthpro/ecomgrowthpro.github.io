Why Etsy listings get ignored

Most Etsy shops I review have good products held back by the same few issues:

  • Titles written for the seller, not for how buyers search
  • Unused tags, or tags that repeat the title
  • Missing attributes Etsy uses to filter results
  • Thumbnails that don't stand out in search
  • Descriptions that leave buyers with unanswered questions
  • Shipping costs and times that put buyers off

Etsy questions

How soon will I see results from Etsy SEO?

Etsy needs time to re-index listings and test them with buyers, so changes usually show over a few weeks rather than overnight. I'll explain what to watch in your Etsy stats while that happens.

Do I need to give you my Etsy password?

No. You can send me your shop link for a review, and for hands-on work we'll agree on a safe way to make changes together.

Can you help with digital products?

Yes. Digital downloads have their own SEO and presentation rules, and I help with keywords, mockups, file delivery and listing setup.

Should I use Etsy Ads?

Sometimes. Ads work best once your listings are optimized. I'll tell you honestly whether they're worth it for your shop and how much to spend.

Can you help me open a Shopify store too?

Yes. Many Etsy sellers add their own Shopify store to build a brand and keep more of each sale. I can set it up and link it to your Etsy shop.
